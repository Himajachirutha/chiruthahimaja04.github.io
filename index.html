AI-Based Resume Screening Platform

Developed by Himaja Chirutha

Project Overview

This platform uses Mistral AI to intelligently screen and rank resumes against job descriptions. It enables bulk resume uploads, AI-driven shortlisting, recruiter dashboards, and subscription-based access control.
